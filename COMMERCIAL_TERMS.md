## 1. Executive Overview

You are welcome and encouraged to:

## 2. Who Needs to Pay the 10% Revenue Share?

### Free / No Revenue Share Required:
- **Personal & Educational Projects**: Learning, university research, student projects, hackathons.
- **Open-Source Contributions**: Developing PRs, testing, debugging, or enhancing the core engine.
- **Internal Evaluation & Prototyping**: Testing Voice Force inside your company before generating revenue or charging clients.
- **Non-Profit Organizations**: Registered 501(c)(3) or charitable organizations using Voice Force for public service or crisis hotlines.

### Commercial Use (10% Revenue Share Applies):
- **SaaS Platforms**: Charging end-users a recurring monthly/annual subscription for an AI phone agent platform.
- **Voice AI Agencies**: Charging clients setup fees, monthly retainers, or per-call management fees for deploying Voice Force agents.
- **Telephony Resellers**: Marking up minutes, SIP trunks, or phone numbers using Voice Force as the underlying dialer/switchboard.
- **Enterprise Internal Commercial Deployment**: Using Voice Force directly to conduct commercial sales/collections at scale where it displaces external paid software.

## 3. Revenue Share Calculation & Definition

### Gross Revenue Definition
**Gross Revenue** is defined as all revenue received by your business from clients for products, subscriptions, or services powered directly or indirectly by Voice Force.

#### Included in Gross Revenue:
- **Platform Subscriptions**: e.g., $99/mo, $299/mo, $999/mo tier plans.
- **Per-Minute Markups**: Any profit margin billed to customers on top of raw telephony costs.
- **One-Time Onboarding / Setup Fees**: Fees charged to set up prompts, knowledge bases, or phone numbers for clients.
- **Custom Integration Fees**: Fees charged to connect client CRMs to Voice Force webhooks.

#### Deductions & Exclusions:
- **Direct Carrier Pass-Through**: If you bill clients for raw Telnyx/Twilio telephony minutes at pure cost without markup, those raw passthrough carrier costs can be excluded upon providing carrier itemized invoices.
- **Refunds & Chargebacks**: Money refunded to customers is deducted from gross revenue calculations.
- **Taxes**: Government sales tax, VAT, or GST collected on behalf of tax authorities.

## 4. Reporting & Remittance Process

1. **Commercial Registration**:
   Before launching your commercial instance or within thirty (30) days of your first paying client, send an email to `partners@vanityworkspace.com` with:
   - Your business / company legal name.
   - Your commercial domain (e.g., `https://callgenius.ai`).
   - Primary contact name, email, and phone number.
   - Estimated monthly call volume and business model.

2. **Monthly Reporting Cycle**:
   - At the conclusion of each calendar month, generate your revenue report (Stripe dashboard export, Razorpay statement, or accounting ledger).
   - Submit your monthly report to `billing@vanityworkspace.com` by the 15th of the following month.

3. **Payment Remittance**:
   - An invoice will be generated for the 10% revenue share.
   - Payments can be remitted via Bank Wire (SWIFT/ACH), Stripe, or Razorpay within 30 days of invoice receipt.
